PixelStow

Put a message, files or a short voice recording inside a photo. Send the encoded image as a birthday card, then open it here to read the message and hear the audio. Everything is processed in this browser and is not uploaded. Text-only images remain compatible with TileStorm Evolution. Use the verified PNG for sharing; resizing and recompression may lose the payload.

Choose a PNG or JPEG, or paste an image. A fingerprint is saved as a new PNG or JPEG, so the original file stays as it is.
