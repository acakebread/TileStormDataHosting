About PixelStow

Everything is processed in this browser and is not uploaded. Encoding saves a new image.

This is a prototype. Short recordings and small attachments work best; heavy payloads can give variable results. Download the verified PNG and keep the original file. Sharing apps may resize or recompress photos, especially large images over about 2,000 pixels, and the hidden contents can disappear. That size is a guideline, not a guaranteed safe limit.

For WhatsApp, send the image as a Document / original file where possible and include the PixelStow opening link. The recipient can save it to Files and use Browse, or copy it and use Paste. A link opens this page but cannot automatically read a WhatsApp attachment. Screenshots, resized copies and ordinary photo sharing may not keep the payload.

On iPhone, allow Safari's Paste prompt after tapping Paste. If needed, use the touch-and-hold paste box. Safari may require a tap on Play audio beside the image before sound is allowed; the full audio player is also available under Attached files. Audio format support varies by browser.

PixelStow automatically attempts recovery from resized, padded, rotated or photographed encoded images. Recovery remains experimental: blur, cropping, print quality and heavy recompression can still prevent reading. Keep the original encoded file where possible.

Capture scans encoded images while the preview stays live. Keep all four picture corners visible and hold steady, or tap the preview or Capture and read to read a frame immediately. On phones, Take photo with phone camera opens the native camera for a sharper still. Photo opens the live camera to take a new source image for encoding. Allow camera access when prompted. The camera closes when you leave the page. All captures and decoding stay on your device.

Encoding formats

Version 5 is the default. It boosts contrast in textured areas while keeping smaller corner markers. Below 5× redundancy, stronger encoding spends more image quality on recovery. Version 4 uses adaptive contrast with balanced markers. Version 3 uses the original eight recovery markers.

Older formats remain available; enable older-format recovery to read them. Photo and resize recovery remain experimental and currently need this browser reader. Choose version 1 for existing desktop and TileStorm readers until they are upgraded; attachments also need binary-payload support.
