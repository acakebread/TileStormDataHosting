Tone and colour recovery

V6 records a 60-byte reference to help restore photographed colours, brightness and contrast. Turn this off to save capacity. This reference does not control wavelet colouring.

Gentle uses the original brightness adjustment for cleaner source recovery. Adjustable contrast offers stronger tone changes. Both support coloured wavelets.
