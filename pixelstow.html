About PixelStow

Everything is processed in this browser and is not uploaded. Encoding saves a new image. The new resize-recovery format currently needs this browser reader. Choose version 1 for existing desktop and TileStorm readers; attachments also need binary-payload support.

This is a prototype. Short recordings and small attachments work best; heavy payloads can give variable results. Download the verified PNG and keep the original file. Sharing apps may resize or recompress photos, especially large images over about 2,000 pixels, and the hidden contents can disappear. That size is a guideline, not a guaranteed safe limit.

For WhatsApp, send the image as a Document / original file where possible and include the PixelStow opening link. The recipient can save it to Files and use Choose image, or copy it and use Paste image. A link opens this page but cannot automatically read a WhatsApp attachment. Screenshots, resized copies and ordinary photo sharing may not keep the payload.

On iPhone, allow Safari's Paste prompt after tapping Paste image. If needed, use the touch-and-hold paste box. Safari may require a tap on Play voice message before sound is allowed; the full audio player is also available under Attached files. Audio format support varies by browser.

PixelStow automatically attempts recovery from resized, padded, rotated or photographed encoded images. Recovery remains experimental: blur, cropping, print quality and heavy recompression can still prevent reading. Keep the original encoded file where possible.

Open camera checks markers in recent captures and reads the most promising frame while the preview stays live. A stronger capture replaces an ongoing read. Keep all four picture corners visible and hold steady, or use Take photo for a sharper still. Live scanning needs HTTPS or localhost and camera permission. The camera closes when you leave the page. All captures and decoding stay on your device.
