How to play

Drag to turn and tilt Eggbot. Right-drag to orbit the camera. Scroll to zoom.

On a touchscreen, use one finger for Eggbot; two fingers to orbit and pinch to zoom.

With the scene focused, use arrow keys to turn Eggbot, Shift + arrows to orbit, + / − to zoom, and 0 to reset the view.

Single animations marked “once” hold their last pose. The sequence repeats Idle → Console Start → Console Loop → Console End.
