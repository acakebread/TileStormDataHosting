Extra notes on technology used in Massive Hadron projects. This page is the place for those details as each one is ready to share.

PixelStow

PixelStow writes a short piece of text into an image as a fingerprint, then reads that text back from the image alone. A password can lock the message. With no password, the text stays public. TileStorm Evolution can read these images, and PixelStow can also write and open them on its own, outside the game.

Encode or decode an image in the browser. The picture stays on this computer and is not uploaded. A link to the PixelStow repository will be added here.
